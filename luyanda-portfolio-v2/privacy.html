Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This website is a personal portfolio run by Luyanda Nkosi (“I”, “me”) in South Africa. I respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). I am the “responsible party” for any information described here.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message, plus the fact that you agreed to this policy. You only provide this if you choose to send a message.
  • Email: if you email me directly, I receive your email address and whatever you write.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (only if you accept): which pages are viewed, which buttons are clicked, your browser, screen size, referrer and approximate country. See “Analytics” below.
  • Technical data: my hosting provider (Netlify) automatically records standard server information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.
  • Your cookie choice: stored in your browser’s local storage so I don’t ask you again.

What I don’t do

  • I do not sell your information.
  • I do not use advertising cookies or build profiles of visitors.

Why I use it

I use your contact details only to read and reply to your message, and to discuss work or opportunities with you. Providing your details is voluntary, but I can’t reply without them.

Analytics

If you click Accept in the cookie banner, I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to see how many people visit and which parts of the site are useful. It sets no cookies, does not track you across other sites and does not store your full IP address in a way that identifies you. If you click Decline, no analytics script is loaded. You can change your mind with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Spam protection

The contact form uses a hidden field, a timing check and Netlify’s spam filtering to block bots. These checks may look at your IP address, browser details and how quickly the form was completed, only to decide whether a submission is spam.

Who it is shared with

  • Netlify hosts this site and processes contact form submissions on my behalf. Their servers may be outside South Africa.
  • GoatCounter receives anonymous usage statistics, only if you accept analytics.
  • Google serves the fonts used on this site (Google Fonts), which means your IP address is sent to Google when the page loads.
  • Email provider: messages are delivered to my email inbox.

I may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long I keep it

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up, and are normally deleted within 12 months. Your cookie choice is remembered for up to 12 months.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, the contact form has spam protection, and no passwords, API keys or other secrets are stored in the website’s front-end code. No online system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your rights

You can ask me to tell you what information I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email Laynkosi1304@gmail.com and I’ll respond as soon as reasonably possible. If you are unhappy with how I handle your information, you can complain to the South African Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Changing your cookie choice

Use the “Cookie settings” link in the footer to change your choice at any time.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy I will update the date at the top of this page.
